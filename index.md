# Letterform and Haiku

## My Letterform

![Three small letters, T, A and S, made of black clay, lying on white paper. The letters are thick, round and soft, like rolled dough.](img/letterform-final.jpg)

I made the letters T, A and S from clay. I rolled the clay into thin snakes and bent them into shapes. Then I painted them with black spray paint.

## My Haiku

Shaping sticky clay,  
Black spray paint covers the lines,  
Sharp letters appear.
